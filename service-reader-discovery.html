Reader Discovery Strategy

Put your book in front of the readers already talking about books like yours.

What’s Included

A Goodreads profile and listing strategy built for genuine reader engagement
A BookTok and TikTok plan matched to your genre and audience
Reader outreach that focuses on relevance over volume
A cross-platform promotion calendar
Guidance on which platforms are actually worth your time, and which are not

Who It’s For

Authors who want to build genuine reader community rather than relying on advertising alone, particularly in genres where reader-driven platforms carry real weight.

The Process

01

Map

We identify where your genre’s readers actually spend their time.

02

Build

We set up or refine your presence on the platforms that matter most.

03

Connect

We develop an outreach approach built on relevance, not mass messaging.

04

Promote

We run a coordinated promotional calendar across chosen platforms.

05

Grow

We track what is working and shift focus toward it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be on every platform?
No. Part of this service is identifying which one or two platforms deserve your focus.
Do you create video content for me?
We provide strategy, direction, and planning. Content creation can be discussed as part of scope.
Is this useful for nonfiction authors?
Yes, though the platform mix and approach differ from fiction and will be tailored accordingly.

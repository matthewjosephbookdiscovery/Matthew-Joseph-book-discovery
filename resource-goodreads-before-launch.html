What to Do on Goodreads Before Your Book Launches

By the time most authors think about Goodreads, their book is already published and the pre-launch window has closed. That is a missed opportunity, because early activity on Goodreads has an outsized effect on how a book performs once reviews start coming in.

The first priority is a complete, accurate author profile and a properly categorized book listing. Genre shelving on Goodreads influences which readers see your book in recommendations, so it deserves the same care as Amazon category selection.

Advance reader copies distributed through Goodreads should be timed carefully. A concentrated wave of early reviews close to launch tends to build more momentum than a slow trickle spread out over months, because it signals current relevance rather than a book that has been quietly available for a while.

Lastly, engagement matters more than promotion. Authors who show up as genuine members of the reading community, not just as people promoting a book, tend to see warmer reception when their own title appears in someone’s feed.

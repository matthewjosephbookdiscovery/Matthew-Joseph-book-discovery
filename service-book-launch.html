Book Launch Strategy Strategy

Give your launch week the strategy it deserves, before it starts.

What’s Included

A full pre-launch timeline covering ARC distribution, reviews, and early buzz
Category and keyword setup so your book is positioned correctly before launch day
A coordinated promotional plan across the platforms that matter for your genre
Launch-day and launch-week execution support
A post-launch plan to carry momentum past the first week

Who It’s For

Authors with an upcoming release who want a coordinated plan instead of a scramble in the final weeks, and authors who have launched before and want the next one to go further.

The Process

01

Assess

We review your manuscript, genre, comparable titles, and timeline.

02

Plan

We build a pre-launch calendar covering ARCs, reviews, categories, and promotion.

03

Coordinate

We line up the promotional pieces so they land in the right order.

04

Launch

We manage execution through launch day and launch week.

05

Sustain

We carry the plan forward so momentum does not stop after week one.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I start?
Ideally eight to twelve weeks before publication, though shorter timelines can still be worked with.
Do you handle ARC distribution directly?
We build and manage the ARC strategy, including where and how copies are distributed for review.
What if this is a re-launch of an existing book?
The same framework applies. A re-launch often benefits from a fresh promotional push and updated positioning.

Pricing Questions

Which package is right for my book?
If your book is already published, Foundation is the most common starting point. If you are also planning ads or a launch, Growth or Premier will fit better. Apply and Matthew will recommend the right fit.
Is advertising spend included?
No. Advertising spend is paid directly to Amazon. The packages cover the strategy, setup, and optimization.
Can a package be customized?
Yes. If your book needs something between two packages, mention it in your application and the scope can be adjusted.
